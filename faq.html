Loona Talk — Frequently Asked Questions

Loona Talk · Loona Automation

Is Loona Talk free?

Yes. Calling Ayan or Anaya on this page is free, with no per-minute charge. Loona Automation separately builds custom voice companions for businesses, but the public companions on this page are free for everyone.

Do I need to sign up?

No account is needed to talk. An optional sign-in lets a companion greet you by name; your name and email are kept in this browser tab for this visit only and are not sent to a server, as the sign-in sheet states.

How does a call work?

Tap Ayan or Anaya and allow microphone access. Your browser turns your speech into text, the text is answered by the live Loona AI engine (the Loona Engine, run by Loona Automation on Make.com, EU1, with an AI model), and your browser speaks the reply aloud. You can also type instead of speaking, read the live transcript, mute, or end the call at any time.

What happens to my voice and what I say?

Your speech is transcribed by your browser and the transcript text is sent to the engine so the companion can reply; the reply text is then spoken by your browser's voice. Because your words are processed to answer you, do not share passwords, bank details or private secrets during a call.

Which languages can I speak?

English, Urdu and Roman Urdu. Ayan and Anaya reply in the same language you are speaking, and you can switch languages mid-conversation.

Why can they not hear me properly?

Loona Talk uses your browser's speech recognition, which works best in Chrome, in a quiet room, at a normal speaking pace. Background noise is the most common cause of mistakes — open the transcript to see what was heard, and check that microphone permission is allowed for this site.

What can Loona Talk not do?

It cannot make phone calls, contact other people, or act on your accounts, and it is not a medical, counselling or emergency service. As the call screen warns, Ayan and Anaya can make mistakes — always double-check important information. It also cannot work offline or without a microphone.
