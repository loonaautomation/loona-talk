About Loona Talk

Loona Talk · Loona Automation

Loona Talk is where Loona stops typing and starts talking: you tap one name — Ayan or Anaya — and a real spoken conversation begins, in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu.

Meet Ayan and Anaya

Ayan and Anaya are Loona Talk's two voice companions. There is no script and no menu of buttons to learn: you just speak, and they talk back, replying in the same language you are speaking. If talking is difficult at any moment you can open the transcript, type your message instead, mute the microphone, or end the call — the call screen keeps all of those controls in reach. An optional sign-in simply lets them greet you by name; as the sign-in sheet itself explains, your name and email are kept in this tab for this visit only and are not sent to a server.

Why a voice companion?

Speaking is the most natural interface there is, and for many people it is the only easy one. Loona Talk is used to practise spoken English or Urdu with a patient partner who never gets tired of repeating, to rehearse answers before an interview or a presentation, to ask questions aloud when typing is slow or difficult, and simply to think out loud with someone who responds. That last point matters to Loona's founding mission — that every book should speak, for everyone who cannot read easily. A conversation you can have entirely by voice asks nothing of your reading or typing at all.

How a call works, step by step

When you tap a companion's name, your browser asks for microphone permission and the call connects to the live Loona AI engine. Your browser's speech recognition turns your voice into text; that text is sent to the Loona Engine, the workflow Loona Automation runs on Make.com (EU1) with an AI model, which writes the companion's reply; your browser then speaks the reply aloud in its own voice. So Loona Talk is not an offline app: it needs the internet, a microphone, and a browser with speech support — Chrome works best. The words you say are transcribed and processed by the engine in order to answer you, so treat a call like a conversation in a public place: enjoy it, but do not share passwords, bank details or private secrets aloud.

Honest limits

The companions themselves carry the warning shown on the call screen: Ayan and Anaya can make mistakes, so always double-check important information. Speech recognition can mishear in noisy rooms or with strong background sound, the spoken voice is your browser's synthetic voice rather than a recorded human, and calls depend on the engine being available — this free version may occasionally connect slowly. Loona Talk is a conversation and practice companion, not a medical, counselling or emergency service.

A companion for your business

As the homepage notes, Loona Automation also builds talking AI companions and voice agents like Ayan and Anaya for clinics, coaching centres, shops and service businesses that miss calls and messages after hours; bookings are open via Fiverr and email. The public Loona Talk page, however, stays a free personal companion for everyone.
