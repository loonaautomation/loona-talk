What to write to us about

Write to us if a companion mishears you constantly, answers in the wrong language, or will not speak aloud; if you use Loona Talk for language practice and want a new practice scenario; or if you run a clinic, coaching centre, shop or service business and want a talking companion like Ayan or Anaya answering your own customers after hours — as the homepage notes, bookings are open on Fiverr and by email.

Try these first — common Loona Talk fixes

The call will not start or the microphone is blocked. Loona Talk needs microphone permission: when the browser asks, choose Allow; if you refused earlier, re-enable the microphone for this site in your browser's site settings, then reload. They cannot understand you. Speech recognition works best in Chrome, in a quiet room, speaking at a normal pace a short distance from the microphone; heavy background noise is the most common cause of wrong transcripts — open the transcript panel to see what was actually heard. You can see replies but hear no voice. Check your device volume and that no silent mode is on; the spoken voice is produced by your browser, so try Chrome if another browser stays silent. Connecting takes a long time. Calls run on the live Loona AI engine, so a slow connection or a busy engine delays the start; try again shortly. They greet you by the wrong name. Use the Account option to update the name kept for this visit, or sign out to remove it.
